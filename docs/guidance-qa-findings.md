# Guidance QA findings (1.15.0 → 1.16.0)

While authoring and replaying the per-objective guidance for all 20 campaign levels, every level was
played in a real browser by a "confused newcomer": staying in `default`, reading the objective label
literally, skipping a prerequisite, fixing too early or too late. This file keeps what that pass found.
**Fixed** items shipped in 1.15.0; **Open** items were deliberately left alone (grading and simulator
behaviour is locked by the 22k-line browser suite, so each one deserves its own change and test).
1.16.0 fixed the open grading list and most of the simulator list, each with its own regression check
(`qa-grading-1160`, `qa-sim-1160`, `scripts/verify.mjs` §4m/§4n); the new levels (Linux prelude 0.1-0.7,
Network Policies, Nodes & Cluster Troubleshooting) were play-tested the same way.

## Fixed in 1.16.2

- **Chaos mode CronJob/Job targets.** A random `CronJobMissedSchedule` with no CronJob or Job bound
  `alertmanager-main-0` (`oc get cronjob alertmanager-main-0`, damage never applied). `JobDeadlineExceeded`
  did the same because Pod is an affected kind. Random spawns no longer borrow a Pod unless the incident
  is about Pods, and create a repairable CronJob or Job when none exists. Locked by `scripts/verify.mjs`
  §4o. A Job that already exists can still be chosen for `CronJobMissedSchedule` (it is an affected kind);
  the empty-cluster case that was seen in play is the one the lock covers.

## Open - simulator fidelity

- **Incident side effects:** a fix made before a scheduled incident re-seeds its value is overwritten
  (L3 `APP_MODE` at t=10 s, L14 RoleBindings at t=25 s, L20 `APP_MODE` at t=60 s) - by design, but silent; the
  how-tos say to fix after the incident appears.
- **Not modelled yet:** a NotReady node's Pods stay Ready and in the Service endpoints (the node lifecycle
  controller would mark them NotReady and evict them after 300 s); MetalLB only assigns an EXTERNAL-IP from
  pools an L2Advertisement covers (real: the controller assigns, the speakers announce - changing it would let
  L12's LoadBalancer objective tick without the L2Advertisement); a client dry run of
  `oc create deployment` prints server-side defaults (strategy, revisionHistoryLimit, a container port) that
  real client-side output omits; `oc explain` / `oc api-resources` still answer after `oc logout` (real oc may
  serve them from its discovery cache - not verified). `oc get bc`/`oc get builds` FROM now follows the
  OCP 4.22 printer (`Git`, `Git@<ref>`, `Git@<commit>`, `Dockerfile`, `Binary`; custom strategy prints the
  custom image name) — fixed in 1.16.1, locked by `scripts/verify.mjs`.

## Replaying guidance

`scripts/browser-test.mjs --only=qa-guidance-replay` starts each level, switches to the lab project the mission
card names, runs every `objective.how.run` / `how.check` through the real command bar (letting the simulation and
incident clocks run, waiting for scheduled incidents before their fix) and requires the level to complete.
Levels that need extra simulated time (operator installs, builds, the authentication-operator rollout) have
settle times in `SETTLE_BY_LEVEL`.
